FLOW DESKTOP CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 0.1.0-beta5
DATE: 2026-11-17
STATUS: PRE-RELEASE

ABOUT
Flow Desktop is a privacy-first YouTube and YouTube Music client - no account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry. It is the desktop companion to Flow for Android.

LINUX
- Fixed Flow staying on a slower graphics mode after 0.1.0-beta3 moved a working computer onto one (#660). The first launch tries the fast mode once and asks whether to keep it.
- Try faster graphics, in Settings > Appearance and in a one-time notice, restarts Flow on a faster graphics mode. If the window stays blank, Flow goes back on its own after 15 seconds.
- On NVIDIA with Wayland, Flow stays on shared memory. The direct GPU path showed a green picture and crashed in testing, so Try faster graphics is not offered there.
- On NVIDIA with WebKitGTK 2.52 or later, Flow no longer moves by itself to drawing without the GPU, which crashes or leaves the window blank on some systems. Installs that climbed there come back on the next launch. Safe and Start with safe graphics still use it when you choose them.
- Fixed sound running on for about a second after the first pause and then staying out of step with the picture (#660). It came from the equalizer and Volume boost on videos: WebKitGTK reads their audio about a second early, and never frees a video that used them. On Linux they are now off for videos and Shorts and show as not available. The equalizer still works for music.
- Flow now raises its open-file limit at start, from the usual 1024 to what the system allows (524288 on most). On NVIDIA's 580 driver the part of Flow that draws the page leaks open files as it redraws, a driver bug that WebKitGTK works around after 2.52, and at 1024 Flow froze and reloaded with "Too many open files" within minutes (#660). The leak itself stays until the driver or WebKitGTK is updated.
- On NVIDIA's 580 driver, Flow now has the page painted by the processor while the GPU still puts the picture together. On a reporter's computer that stopped the leak of open files: the count stayed near 130 where it used to reach 1024 after a few window resizes, and video was no less smooth (#660). Start Flow with WEBKIT_SKIA_ENABLE_CPU_RENDERING=0 to turn it off.
- The Linux help page now says to run flatpak update after an NVIDIA driver update, so the driver inside the Flatpak matches the system's.
- Starting Flow with your own WebKitGTK renderer variables now really uses them. After a start that failed, Flow used to add its own setting next to yours, which kept shared memory on, and it remembered a crashed test as a failed start. Such a start is now left alone and not remembered, and Settings > About says so.
- Flow on Linux asks for fewer redraws: the playing bars and live dots hold still, pages and the side menu switch without a fade or slide, and the video has square corners. Ambient mode stays off on the slower graphics modes.
- On Linux, a card now changes at once when the pointer moves onto it, with no fade. The fade made WebKitGTK rebuild the whole card as separate layers and draw about a dozen extra frames each time the pointer moved onto or off a card, also while a feed scrolled under it. In a test of 24 hovers that was 669 frames with the fades and 85 without (#660). Cards in a shelf no longer lift, and pictures on playlist, channel and artist cards no longer grow, which made a card shift by a few pixels under the pointer.
- Seeking inside the loaded part of a video, and fast quality switches, no longer make WebKitGTK restart its decoder, which could stutter.
- The video no longer goes black for a moment when you seek or change the speed (#696). Flow keeps the last picture on screen, zoomed as it was, until the new one is ready.
- The Device rows in Settings > About, such as Graphics and Video decoders, can be selected and copied.
- Settings > About now names the graphics path in use (GPU, GPU through shared memory, or no GPU) and why. The Linux help page explains what each one costs.
- Settings > Appearance > Paint the page on the processor (Automatic, Processor, Graphics card) lets you choose where the page is drawn without starting Flow from a terminal, including in the Flatpak. Try Processor if pictures go black or flicker while the text stays. Automatic keeps doing it on NVIDIA driver 580 only.
- The Graphics line in Settings > About and in bug reports says when the page is painted on the processor and names the GPUs Flow can draw with, so a hybrid laptop shows as, for example, GPU amd + nvidia.
- The AppImage now registers itself for flow:// links again, so videos sent from the browser extension open in Flow. Registering ran the system's xdg-mime with the AppImage's own libraries, which made the system shell fail with undefined symbol: rl_print_keybinding (#770). Flow now runs it with the system's libraries, as it already did for opening links and folders.

HOME AND RECOMMENDATIONS
- Home no longer redraws every card each time the feed loads more or changes state. Loading a page sends only its new videos, where it used to resend the whole feed at least three times.
- Hiding a video on Home no longer reloads every card after it.
- When Home can't load and has nothing saved, it now lists your downloaded videos and songs under the error, so there is still something to play offline.
- When the connection comes back, Home offers Back online · Refresh above the cards instead of swapping them while you read. A Home that failed with nothing on screen loads by itself.
- When Home or a chip can't load, it now says why: YouTube blocking your network, a bot check or a rate limit get their own message and what to try (#770). Check your connection is kept for when Flow is really offline.
- Refreshing Home now brings new videos and new Shorts (#785). Refresh, or clicking Home at the top of the page, asks YouTube for new related videos instead of reusing lists up to 45 minutes old, and the Shorts shelf loads again with the feed.
- Videos you just saw on Home go behind new ones after a refresh, instead of all coming back whenever fewer than 20 new videos were found. Refreshing again within a minute reorders what Flow already has without asking YouTube again.

SEARCH
- Search suggestions keep up with typing. A space after a word no longer asks YouTube again, and a request that fails or takes longer than 2 seconds leaves the rows you had instead of an empty list.
- YouTube Music suggestions are no longer retried after a failure. A retry arrived after you had typed on, and a slow one could hold a connection for up to 30 seconds.
- A past search now shows once in suggestions, even when it was typed with extra spaces.
- Search suggestions now follow your content language in Settings. They were always asked for in English, while the results came back in your language.
- The first suggestion after a pause comes faster. Clicking into an empty search box opens the connection to YouTube's suggestion server, which cut the first answer from about 365 to 157 milliseconds in a test.
- Clear all beside your recent searches, in the search box and on the search page, now asks first, as Settings already did. One click used to remove every saved search with its filters.
- Search, channels, Explore, Home and the music pages load again by themselves when the connection comes back, once per reconnect. They used to stay on their error until you clicked Retry.
- The music pages say when you are offline, rate limited or stopped by a bot check, instead of showing the error text from YouTube's servers.
- Long searches work, such as a full homework question pasted into the search box (#778). Flow refused anything over 200 characters. YouTube searches now take up to 3,000 characters and YouTube Music searches up to 5,000, and any text past that is left out instead of showing an error.
- Search suggestions stop after 100 characters, where YouTube's suggestion server stops answering, instead of asking it on every key.
- Settings > Search and history now has three pause switches, all off by default: Pause watch history, Pause search history and Pause search suggestions (#784). They replace Save search history and Search suggestions, and your choices carry over.
- Pausing search history now stops new searches being saved and keeps your past searches listed until you clear them. Turning saving off used to hide them all.

SUBSCRIPTIONS AND CHANNELS
- Subscriptions opens on its first 120 videos and adds more as you scroll, instead of drawing up to 1500 cards at once. Coming back to the page returns to where you were.
- While a first check of your channels runs, videos already listed are no longer redrawn with every batch.
- Channel pictures on cards, in the side menu and in lists are loaded at the size they are shown, not at 512 pixels each.
- Cards look up their DeArrow title and channel picture only once they come near the screen, and no longer ask the backend for an image they never show.
- A post that shares a playlist now shows the playlist as a card you can open, as on Flow for Android. Such posts used to show only their text.
- The channels in the side rail follow the order chosen in Subscriptions > Manage (by name or by latest upload) instead of the order you followed them in.
- Pin channels to the top with the pin button in Subscriptions > Manage, by right clicking a channel in the side rail, or from the Subscribed menu on a channel. Pinned channels lead the side rail and the channel row on Subscriptions, always show even past the first seven, and drag into the order you want. Pins sync with Flow for Android.
- A new Your order sort in Subscriptions > Manage lets you drag every channel into your own order, and the side rail follows it. Channels you follow later go at the end.
- Subscriptions > Manage has a Most watched sort, as in Flow for Android: the channels you have watched the most videos from come first, in Manage and in the side rail.

CARDS
- Upload dates on cards, such as "3 days ago", now follow the app's language instead of the computer's, and "Streamed" and "Premiered" can be translated.
- The views and date line on a card now wraps only after the dot, so it no longer ends a line on "3 days" or "1.2M".
- Hiding a video in search grids, on a channel, in Explore or in a shelf no longer reloads every card after it.
- Video thumbnails are now loaded at the size the card draws them. A related video or a list row on a Retina or HiDPI screen loads a 320 or 480 pixel picture instead of the 1280 pixel one, and grids, shelves and search rows pick the smallest size that stays sharp.
- Thumbnails load as WebP where YouTube has a copy, which was 29 to 56% smaller on the videos we measured, and as JPEG otherwise. A size a video doesn't have is skipped for the rest of the session instead of being asked for again each time its card appears.
- Fixed a playlist or mix whose first video is an old upload showing a broken cover or YouTube's grey placeholder. The cover now steps down to a size the video has.
- Playlist and mix cards have a new stack: the two sheets behind the cover are now the theme's own surface colours, tinted with the cover's colour once it loads, as on Flow for Android. They used to be two blurred copies of the cover under a black shade, which looked dark in the light theme. A playlist without a cover now shows the stack too.
- The number of videos on a playlist card, and the word Playlist when there is no count, now follow the app's language.
- Post cards in a shelf, on a channel's Home and in search, are now all the same size. The text is shortened and a picture, video or playlist shows as a small square at the side. Clicking a card opens the whole post with its comments.
- Shelves no longer show how many items they hold next to their title.
- The Continue Watching shelf on Home has a View all button that opens History.
- See all, Show all and View all on every shelf are now a rounded button, as on the Library page. More like this in a card's menu has a thumbs up icon instead of sparkles.

PLAYER
- The description is laid out like Flow for Android's: the likes, views and date as three tiles, the text with YouTube's own formatting, links to other videos as cards, then hashtags, credited songs, how the video was made, Chapters and Transcript rows that open their panel, the channel with its links, and the tags.
- Links in descriptions and comments look like Android's: channel and site links sit on rounded chips with the site's icon, moments and hashtags are bold, and web links are underlined.
- Settings > Player > Tint the description colors the description's cards and links from the video's thumbnail. It is off by default.
- The player, its controls and its menus no longer redraw several times a second while a video plays. Only the parts that show the time follow it.
- The transcript panel redraws when the spoken line changes instead of on every tick, and a Short no longer redraws its whole surface as it plays.
- The buffered part of the seek bar keeps growing while a video is paused.
- The seek bar, the mini player bar and the music bar are redrawn only when they move by a pixel, not on every frame. On a long video that is a few times a second instead of 60 or more.
- The mini player's bar and the seek bar's thumb are no longer animated while they are hidden.
- When the controls hide, the seek bar slides down to the bottom edge and stretches across the whole video instead of fading out and leaving a separate plain bar. It keeps its chapter gaps and SponsorBlock marks, and slides back up when you move the pointer over the video.
- Auto quality now stops at the size that fills the player on your screen. Fullscreen on a 4K screen still reaches 2160p, while a small player or the mini player no longer loads and decodes a picture larger than it shows. A quality you pick yourself is never limited.
- On Linux, Auto stays on H.264 and stops at 1080p, since video there is mostly decoded on the processor. Pick a higher quality by hand to go above it.
- Fixed Auto starting far too high after a video had played through the backup stream: its segments were timed as if they came over the network, which made the connection look hundreds of times faster than it was.
- When the player has to switch to a backup stream, the saved log now says why.
- Video already played is dropped from memory every 30 seconds instead of every 2 minutes.
- The dark shade behind the player controls is lighter and covers only the bottom band they sit in, not the whole picture, and that band gets shorter in a small player (#719). The video stays bright while the controls show, and the shade costs less to draw.
- Settings > Player > Shade behind the controls picks Standard, Light or Off (#719). With Off, the buttons, time and title get darker backings instead, so they stay readable on a bright video.
- Right-click the video and choose Copy frame to put the picture on screen on the clipboard, or Save frame to keep it as a PNG named after the video and the moment (#658). The frame keeps the video's own resolution.
- The pop-out window's title and buttons sit on dark backings, like the mini player's, so they stay readable over a bright video (#664). They also show when you reach them with the keyboard.
- Swipe sideways with two fingers over a video to move back or forward through it, as in QuickTime Player, and turn the wheel over the seek bar to move by the seek step. Both follow the Trackpad gestures switch in Settings > Keyboard.
- Zoom into a video with a trackpad pinch or Ctrl (Cmd on macOS) and the wheel, then drag or scroll with two fingers to move around it (#700). Z zooms in, Shift+Z zooms out, X goes back to the whole picture, and W, A, S and D move a zoomed video. Zoom goes from 1x to 6x, as on Android, and stops for a moment where the picture fills the screen.
- A short read-out shows the zoom level, and the right-click menu has Zoom in, Zoom out and Reset zoom. Copy frame and Save frame keep only the zoomed part, at the video's own resolution. Captions keep their size, and each new video starts at 1x.
- Settings > Player > Wheel over the video picks what the mouse wheel or a two-finger scroll does with the pointer on the video: scroll the page as before, change the volume, or zoom. When it zooms, Ctrl (Cmd on macOS) and the wheel change the volume instead (#700).
- Space and Enter on a button you reached with Tab now press that button instead of pausing the video, and the arrow keys on a focused slider or seek bar no longer also seek or change the volume. A button clicked with the mouse still leaves Space to the player, and the same now holds in Shorts and the music player.
- The buffering circle waits a fifth of a second before it shows, so a short refill after a seek or speed change no longer flashes it.
- The backings behind the player's buttons, time and chapter pills are darker, so they stay readable over a bright video. Standard goes to 35%, Light to 45% and Off to 55%.
- Loop in the player (right click, the R key, and a new switch in the gear menu) now repeats the video until you turn it off or close the player, as in Flow for Android. It used to turn on the saved Loop every video setting, which then looped every later video and reached your phone through sync. Loop every video stays in Settings > Player.
- When Google blocks your network for unusual traffic, a video now says YouTube is blocking this network, how long that usually lasts and what to try, instead of saying it couldn't read the stream (#770). Flow also stops at the first block page, where it used to ask about ten more times from the same blocked address.

COMMENTS
- Oldest now starts from the oldest comment. YouTube has no oldest-first order, so Flow reads the Newest order to its end, up to about 500 comments on big videos, and turns it around. Past that it says how far it reached.
- Oldest used to sort only the comments already loaded and read their age in English, so in other languages the order was wrong.
- Community post comments now switch between Top, Newest and Oldest, and a comment YouTube sends twice shows once.
- The watch page and the fullscreen comments panel now share one list, so comments load once and threads you opened stay open after the panel closes.
- Replies are now drawn as the conversation they are. YouTube sends them as one flat list, so Flow reads the @name a reply starts with and puts it under the earlier reply it answers, with lines from each avatar, as Flow for Android does.
- Threads indent up to three steps; replies deeper than two levels fold behind "N replies" until you open them. Right-to-left languages get the same lines mirrored.
- The creator's heart on a comment now shows the creator's avatar with a red heart on it, and a thread the creator answered shows their avatar next to its reply count.
- Bold, italic and struck-through text in comments keeps its style. Long comments stop at four lines with Read more, and keep their links and custom emoji while shortened.
- GIFs attached to comments and replies now show under their text. GIFs in comments, in Settings > Content, turns them off and syncs with Flow for Android.
- Timed now opens with YouTube's own Timed list in one request, about a third of a second in a test, where it used to read up to 16 pages. Each comment sits at the time YouTube gives it. Videos without such a list still read pages as before.
- Shorts comments now load from the Short's own comment panel instead of its full watch page, as Flow for Android does. A Short without that panel still loads through the watch page.
- More comments load as you scroll to the end of the list, with Retry if a page fails. Timed keeps its Load more button.

MUSIC
- Fixed the full music player sliding under the title bar on Windows and Linux and leaving a coloured strip along the bottom of the window (#693). It now opens just below the title bar and fills the rest of the window.
- Album, song and artist cards on music shelves, and song rows, now load their artwork at the size they are drawn instead of at 1080 pixels. A song whose artwork is its video's thumbnail uses the 320 pixel picture in a row.
- Pictures in channel posts load at the size of the post, where they always asked for 2048 pixels.

KEYBOARD AND GESTURES
- The keyboard shortcuts list (press ? or open Settings > Keyboard) now shows every key in Flow, including Shorts, tabs and windows, Back and Forward, and search. On macOS it shows keys the way the Mac menus do, such as ⇧⌘N.
- Change any shortcut in Settings > Keyboard: click a key and press the new one, add a second key with +, or reset one or all. If the key is already taken, Flow names the command that has it and offers to move it. Your keys are kept in Flow backups.
- Single-key shortcuts in Settings > Keyboard turns off keys such as K, M and ? that act without Ctrl, Alt or Cmd, for speech input or to avoid pressing them by accident.
- New keys: / or Ctrl+K (Cmd+K) to search, Ctrl+, (Cmd+,) for Settings, Ctrl+B (Cmd+B) to show or hide the side rail, and G then a letter to go to a page, such as G then H for Home or G then S for Subscriptions.
- Ctrl+R (Cmd+R) and F5 refresh Home, Subscriptions, Explore and Music instead of reloading the whole app, so playback no longer stops.
- Ctrl+Shift+T (Cmd+Shift+T) reopens the last closed tab, and Ctrl+Page Up or Page Down (Alt+Cmd+Left or Right on macOS) move between tabs.
- More player keys: R loops the video, O switches between Fit, Fill and Zoom, Shift+S saves the frame, Shift+I shows Stats for nerds, Shift+L likes and Shift+W saves to Watch later. In the music player Shift+L likes the song and Y shows the lyrics, and in Shorts Shift+L likes and C opens the comments.
- Copy frame, Copy link at this moment, Normal speed, Chapters, Dislike, Save to a playlist and Equalizer can also get keys of your own in Settings > Keyboard.
- K, M, Shift+N and Shift+P now play or pause, mute and skip music while only the music bar shows. Before, music answered keys only with the full player open.
- Turn the wheel over the music player's progress bar to move by the seek step. Shift and the wheel still move by 5 seconds.
- Video and playlist cards can now be reached with Tab. On any card the arrow keys move to the next card that way, across grids and shelves, Enter opens it and Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter) opens it in a new tab.
- Settings > Appearance > Interface size makes everything in Flow larger or smaller, from 67% to 200%. Ctrl and = or - (Cmd on macOS) change it from anywhere, and Ctrl+0 (Cmd+0) goes back to 100%.
- Swipe sideways with two fingers on a trackpad to go back or forward, as in Safari and Edge. An arrow fills in at the edge as you swipe. Rows that scroll sideways and the player keep their own swipes, and Settings > Keyboard > Trackpad gestures turns it off.
- Pinch a feed on a trackpad to change how many thumbnails share a row, from 2 to 8, as in Photos. Elsewhere outside the player a pinch does nothing.

BACKUPS AND IMPORT
- Fixed Flow closing while it read a Google Takeout export with a folder named in Russian, Ukrainian, Greek, Turkish, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese or Korean, such as My Activity (#661).

SYNC
- Try again on the keychain card now asks the keychain again. Before, a keychain that could not be reached once, for example KDE Wallet starting after Flow, stayed unreachable until Flow restarted (#659). Last.fm's Allow access button gets the same fix.
- The keychain card says what went wrong and what to do on your system: on Linux it names KDE Wallet and GNOME Keyring, and it mentions macOS only on a Mac.
- Flow no longer opens the keychain at launch on a device that is in no sync group.
- A computer removed from a sync group on the local network now finds out the next time it reaches a desktop still in the group, and stops syncing, instead of showing Linked forever (#662). That desktop gives it only the list of devices.
- New installs name themselves after the computer, such as Flow Desktop (MY-PC - Windows), so two installs in one group are easy to tell apart. Development builds add Dev.
- When a sync server or WebDAV folder can't be reached, Flow now says why: nothing answered, the port refused the connection, the name wasn't found, the server stopped answering, or the address isn't a web server (Flow for Android #2233).
- A WebDAV folder address that the server redirects, doesn't have, or doesn't allow writing to now gets its own message, and each failed request is written to the log.
- When two linked devices share a name, the device list and the remove confirmation add the start of each device id, and each device offered for removal shows when it last synced, so removing one no longer risks removing its twin (#2184).
- A device removed on the local network now finds out straight away when it is reachable, instead of staying Linked until it next reaches a member. The devices left in the group get the removal at once too.
- Pinned channels sync between Flow Desktop and Flow for Android. Your own channel order syncs between desktops.

DESKTOP
- The update dialog lists only what changed since the version you have. Skipped a release? You see each one since yours, newest first. A nightly shows just the runs after the one you have, merged into one list, instead of everything since the last release.
- The update dialog is redesigned like Flow for Android's update page: the new version and what it replaces, the release date, the notes in sections, and the download running inside the main button. Skip this version and View on GitHub sit in its menu.
- Flow starts with a smaller first script: Settings, Sync, Recap, your Persona, Extensions, Diagnostics, Import, Support and the welcome screens now load when they are first opened.
- Cards and panels across the app no longer have an outline. They sit on the same fill as the settings rows, so pages like Your taste, Taste and Appearance look lighter. Dialogs, menus and dividers keep a softer line, and chips, fields and outlined buttons keep theirs.
- On Linux and Windows, Settings > Appearance > Window buttons can move the minimize, maximize and close buttons into the top bar, which removes the title bar and lets the empty part of the top bar move the window, or hide them for tiling window managers. Onboarding and the full music player keep the title bar when the buttons are in the top bar.
- On macOS 26 and later, the Flow icon now sits on its own white rounded square instead of the gray plate macOS added behind it, in the Dock, Finder and Launchpad. Windows and Linux keep the logo on its own.
- Flow Nightly has its own icon, as on Flow for Android: the logo with a moon badge, on an indigo square on macOS. Its Windows notifications show that icon too. Flow and Flow Beta keep the usual one.
- YouTube links you click in your browser can open in Flow (#777). Turn on Open YouTube links in Flow in the Flow browser extension: videos, Shorts and songs then open here, the window comes to the front, and the browser tab goes back to where you were. Settings > Content > Links has the download link.
- For Safari and other browsers without the extension, Settings > Content > Links has an Open in Flow bookmark that sends the YouTube page you are on to Flow.
- A YouTube link opened in Flow now starts at the time it points to, such as youtu.be/...?t=90 or &t=1m30s.

DOWNLOADS
- The Downloads page no longer redraws every saved video and song four times a second while something is downloading.
- The download cards in the corner no longer re-measure the page on every progress update.

FIXES
- Keyboard shortcuts now work with an Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Russian, Greek or other non-Latin keyboard layout. K, J, L, M, F, the tab keys and Ctrl or Cmd+K are read by where they sit on the keyboard, as on YouTube.
- Fixed hidden helper windows staying open until Flow quit when a video took too long to resolve, which made Flow use more memory the longer it ran.
- Preparing playback for one video no longer waits behind a slow token request for another.
- Fixed covers and artwork turning into placeholders after Flow had been open for about six hours.
- Flow lets go of expired stream links and of a finished video's buffer on its own every minute, where it used to wait for the next video to be opened.
- Opening Diagnostics & logs no longer freezes the window while a week of logs is read.
- Scrolling a feed no longer rewrites your whole recommendation profile to disk every three seconds. What a feed showed is now saved once a minute, with the next thing you watch, or when Flow closes.
- Undoing a deleted note now always wins over the deletion, even when both happen in the same instant.
- Fixed a possible freeze after hours of use when Flow was started by a launcher that never reads its output.
- Extension settings such as Discord presence, SponsorBlock and DeArrow now start with their saved values at launch and after a sync. A second load that started while the first was still running used to read the defaults (#763).

UNDER THE HOOD
- The check for a video's paid promotion label now asks YouTube for only the two fields it reads, as it was meant to. A missing separator in the request address made YouTube send the whole player answer, about 238 KB, where 37 bytes do.
- A first component, the video card, is built with the React Compiler, which only compiles components that opt in.
- While Google blocks your network for unusual traffic, Flow stops the requests you didn't ask for: Home's work after its first page, prefetches, hover previews and the new-upload check. They wait 15 minutes, then 30, then an hour if the block is still there (#770). Playing, searching and Refresh still ask YouTube, and the first answer from YouTube ends the wait.
