FLOW DESKTOP CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 0.1.0-beta4
DATE: 2026-10-28
STATUS: PRE-RELEASE

ABOUT
Flow Desktop is a privacy-first YouTube and YouTube Music client - no account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry. It is the desktop companion to Flow for Android.

PROFILES
- Up to five profiles on one computer, each with its own recommendations, history, library and settings, so a study profile and a gaming profile never mix. Downloads are shared.
- Switch from your avatar in the top bar or in Settings > Profiles. Switching restarts Flow, and the new profile opens in its own theme.
- A new profile starts fresh with a short setup, or copies your settings, subscriptions or recommendations. Profiles are not locked: anyone using the computer can switch.
- Only the Default profile syncs with your phone. A master backup now holds every profile and restores them all.
- The recommendation engine's profile is now called your taste profile.
- With two or more profiles, Flow can ask who's watching when it opens. Turn Ask who's watching off in Settings > Profiles.
- First-run setup now ends with a summary and adds two steps: Artists, where artists you pick shape your music picks only, and Alerts, for new video alerts.

SYNC
- Restoring with your recovery key now asks which devices you still have, and signs out the rest before it syncs anything new, so a lost device can't read what comes next.
- Your recommendations sync with Flow for Android again. Both apps now read Chinese titles the same way, folding Traditional characters into Simplified ones.
- When something stays on each device, the sync result now says what and why. After receiving, it lists what arrived.
- SponsorBlock's action for each segment type now syncs with your phone. Synced SponsorBlock and DeArrow switches apply without a restart.
- Data synced before comes back if this computer lost it. Music playlists from your phone no longer return under a new name, history sent to the phone has thumbnails, and subscription groups are sent when Flow Desktop scans the phone's code.
- Play on: moving playback between Flow Desktop and your phone works again. A phone that paused or stopped no longer stays listed as playing, and a phone at full volume no longer shows its volume at 0.
- Play on: picking a device now works as on Android. This computer takes playback back from the device you control, picking another device moves that device's session there, and a device that stops answering stops showing as playing.
- Linked devices: choose which profiles sync. A profile starts its own synced profile or joins one from your other devices, and Profiles under Linked devices adds one that only your other devices have.
- A synced profile that isn't open keeps what arrives and catches up when you switch to it. Stopping a profile on every device keeps its data on each one.
- Linked devices has a Dashboard: where the group lives, how much of a Flow server's storage it uses, your devices, each synced profile's state and the latest sync errors.
- The Flow Neuro page can show the interests of your other synced profiles, read on this computer without switching. From the Dashboard, View interests opens them.
- A sync that arrives while Flow is busy saving something else, such as right after switching profiles, no longer fails and has to be run again.
- A new device's Default profile now syncs only with Default here. If Default doesn't sync on this computer, the new device's Default starts its own synced profile instead of merging into another one.
- On the local network, a device list entry from another device that can't be read is skipped, and the rest of the list still syncs.
- Linked devices: a device name that can't be read is left out instead of hiding the device list or stopping a rename. The latest readable name is shown.
- A snapshot, acknowledgement or LAN request that names a log position too large to read exactly is now refused as invalid.
- Linked devices accept only device keys and signatures that every Flow app reads the same way, so devices can't disagree about who is in a group.
- A damaged file in a WebDAV folder's roster no longer stops every device from syncing. It is skipped and the rest of the roster is read.
- Sync no longer hangs on a store that keeps answering with the same page. It stops reading and tries again at the next sync.
- Sync stops reading an answer larger than it can be, from a Flow server, a WebDAV folder, the live channel or a device on your network, so a broken or hostile store can't use up memory.
- A damaged or forged object from one device no longer fails the whole sync. Reading that device's changes pauses there, and the other devices' changes still arrive.
- The same holds for an object or a file in a store's folder that is too large or that the store refuses: only that device's changes pause. A store that is out of reach, refuses your login or asks to slow down still fails the sync.
- The same holds when a new device starts from a snapshot or a profile starts syncing: a damaged or forged object no longer fails the sync, and the profile is caught up once it can be read.
- A snapshot or profile name from another device that carries an out-of-range number no longer stops a synced profile from being read.
- Linked devices accept a synced object's data only in the one form every Flow app writes, so two devices can't read different data from the same object.
- A malformed roster entry from another device on the local network is refused, so the group stays readable on this computer.
- Linked devices refuse a device list entry whose sealed device name isn't in the one form every Flow app writes.
- Joining a group over the local network now refuses a malformed welcome from the other device.
- Linked devices: Replace recovery words gives your group a new recovery key from any linked device. The old words stop working, and your devices move to a new encryption key.
- The Sync page now opens on Linked devices, which keep your devices in sync all the time. The one-time transfer to a nearby device moves below them, and Linked devices stay on screen while a transfer runs.
- On the local network, an object as large as a sync object can be is now passed between devices whole, instead of being refused for its size.
- Linked devices check for devices asking to join every 5 seconds only while the Sync page is on screen, and the check no longer reads the whole device list from the server.
- A new device that starts from a snapshot whose data can't be read now starts from an older snapshot instead of failing the sync.
- Linked devices: allowing or declining remote control now takes effect whichever profile is open.
- Linked devices reach a Flow server or WebDAV folder on your local network directly, even when a system proxy is set.
- Linked devices only ever touch files inside the group's own WebDAV folder.
- One device's unreadable change no longer keeps other devices' changes from arriving, however many are waiting.
- When the store refuses something this computer wrote, Linked devices keep receiving changes, retry it in order and show the error, instead of stopping the sync.
- On the local network, a connection that stays silent is dropped after a few seconds, and it no longer stops this computer from meeting your other devices or answering them.
- Linked devices sign in to your Flow server about once every 15 minutes instead of before every sync.
- Notes sync with Flow for Android, over the QR code and Linked devices, and are part of backups. Notes your phone sent before this version come back on the next sync, and a device that joins later gets them from this computer.
- Saved Shorts sync with your phone in both directions, as the same list on each device, once the phone has saved a Short of its own. Saved Shorts that an earlier sync brought in as a playlist move to the Saved Shorts page.

LIBRARY
- Notes on videos and channels: a Note button on the Watch and Channel pages, a small card that shows the note, and Note at this time in the player's settings. Times like 12:34 in a video's note jump there. Settings > Content has the switches.
- A Notes page in Library and the sidebar: search, filter by videos or channels, sort by edit time, title or your own order, and select notes to copy, delete or reorder. Each note lists its times, and Play from starts the video there.

MUSIC
- Autoplay radio no longer stops after about 20 songs. A station keeps adding songs while it has more to play.
- Instrumental songs no longer show KuGou's notice as their lyrics.
- Clicking an empty part of the music bar now opens the full player. The buttons, the seek line and dragging up work as before.
- Hiding or showing the Queue or Lyrics pane in the full player no longer stretches the title and controls, and the artwork moves with the text.
- A new Blur other lines switch in the lyrics menu and Settings keeps every line of synced lyrics sharp. Linux never blurs them, so it doesn't show there.
- Synced lyrics stay sharp while you scroll them by hand. Lines no longer cross when you click one or seek, and a line you click moves straight into place.
- Follow on an artist's page now really follows them. It used to forget as soon as you left the page. Artists you follow, here or on an artist card, go to your phone as music artists.
- A song played from search now plays alone, with radio filling up next, instead of queueing the other results.
- Album and playlist pages have a search, and each one remembers how you sorted it: title, artist, album, length or, on your own lists, date added.
- Select songs on an album or playlist to play them next, queue them, add them to an album, download them or take them out of your own list with Undo.
- Rename your own albums and change their description, download whole YouTube Music playlists, share albums and playlists, and get suggestions under your own lists.
- Settings > Music has a switch for each music home shelf. New shelves show your own playlists and the artists you follow, plus Live performances and Music videos for you when YouTube offers them.
- Import your top artists from Last.fm, Libre.fm or ListenBrainz in Settings > Music, so recommendations start from your real listening.
- If you're signed in to Last.fm, the music home gets a Discover with Last.fm shelf picked from what you have on repeat.
- Artist pages show how often you've played an artist and whether you liked them, with your most played songs of theirs under Your history.
- Artist pages also get a Featured on shelf and a Share button.
- Crossfade can also blend when you skip a song, can fade between tracks of the same album, and has a choice of fade curves: Equal power, Smooth, Hold and Gentle.
- Animated artwork: turn it on in Settings > Music and albums with motion artwork loop it over the cover in the full player.
- Romanize on this device, in Settings > Music and the lyrics menu, adds Latin letters under lyrics in other scripts when the source has none. Japanese isn't covered yet.

PLAYER
- Scroll over any volume control to change the volume: the video player, Shorts, the music bar, the full music player and Play on.
- While muted, scrolling up unmutes to your saved volume and scrolling down leaves it alone.
- Moving the music volume while muted no longer leaves the song silent.
- Ambient mode now uses the same glow as Flow for Android, and it moves as one smooth image with no pulsing or lines.
- A new switch, Glow around the player, spreads those colors past the player in every view. It is off by default, and it has its own brighter look in light themes.
- Double-clicking the sides of a video seeks without pausing it first or flashing the pause icon. A single click now pauses about a fifth of a second later, so Flow can tell the two apart.
- Ambient mode can be turned on or off from the player gear menu, has an intensity slider in Settings, and can now be turned on under Linux, where it starts off.
- Live streams start and play again.
- You can seek back through a live stream and stay there. The time shows how far behind live you are, and the Live button brings you back.
- A live stream that falls behind from buffering catches up on its own, unless you chose to seek back.
- Drag the mini player anywhere in the window. It stays where you put it, including after a restart, and keeps its place when the window is resized.
- Videos can now play above 1080p: 1440p, 4K and 8K in the quality menu really play at that resolution, using VP9 or AV1.
- Auto now climbs as high as your connection allows, up to the top quality of the video. It keeps to one codec and leaves HDR for when you pick it yourself.
- A default quality of 1440p or higher is now reached even with H.264 as the preferred codec, and 8K can be chosen as the default. Your phone gets 4K for that setting.
- A new switch in Settings, Switch quality quickly, shows a new quality within a few seconds instead of after the loaded video plays out. It is off by default.
- Videos now start at the quality you chose instead of looking soft for the first few seconds. This can make the first picture appear a little later. On a slower connection a video still starts quickly at a lower quality and steps up after a few seconds.
- Auto now starts at a quality your connection can carry, based on the last videos you played.
- When a video can't be decoded in AV1 or VP9, Flow carries on from the same spot in another codec, and remembers that for the video. This replaces about 30 seconds of black screen and a switch to a different source.
- Preferred Codec in Settings greys out codecs this device can't play. A codec that keeps failing is greyed out too, with a Try again button, and comes back by itself after 30 days.
- SponsorBlock now works in the pop-out player and for the next video in the mini player.
- If SponsorBlock can't be reached, the next video plays without skips instead of skipping at the last video's times.
- Rewinding to before a skipped segment skips it again, the same as Flow for Android. To watch it, seek inside the segment.
- A video that falls back to its backup stream now keeps the audio you chose, usually the original, instead of playing an auto-dubbed language. The audio menu works on that stream too.
- Original titles, in Settings under Content, keeps video titles in the video's own language instead of YouTube's automatic translation, on cards, Shorts, the queue and the watch page. It is off by default, and DeArrow titles still come first when DeArrow is on. A single video you download is named the same way (#377).
- Audio Track Language in Settings now works. Videos with several audio tracks start in your language, and fall back to the original when it isn't offered. The choice also syncs with your phone.
- Autoplay is now two switches, as on Android. Autoplay queued videos keeps a playlist or queue going, and Autoplay related videos decides whether a suggestion plays after it, so a queue can run on with no random videos.
- An optional Autoplay delay shows an Up next card with Cancel, Restart and Play now. It is off by default, and the player's settings menu has an Autoplay switch.
- Comments now open in the fullscreen side panel, from a new button on the fullscreen controls.
- Comments can be sorted by Oldest and Timed. Timed lists comments by the moment in the video they point at, and Follow the video moves the list along as it plays.
- Remember speed per channel starts each channel at the speed you last chose, and Play music at normal speed starts music videos at 1x. A channel's speed comes first, and neither changes the default for the next video.
- The player's settings menu has Resize with Fit, Fill and Zoom. Shift+N plays the next video and Shift+P the previous one.
- SponsorBlock has every action Android has: Skip automatically once, Show a skip button, Notify, Mute, Show in seek bar and Disable. Your choices sync with your phone.
- SponsorBlock off for this video, in the player's settings menu, turns it off until the next video.
- New SponsorBlock types Hook and Highlight. A highlight is marked on the seek bar and can offer Jump to highlight as the video starts. Segments submitted as a mute are muted instead of skipped.
- Subtitle timing moves captions earlier or later in steps, for each video. Add an SRT or VTT file from the subtitles menu or by dropping it on the player, and Flow remembers it for that video.
- Subtitle style adds window color and opacity, edge styles, custom colors and a position for fullscreen.
- Preferred subtitle language now lists every language and has No preference, which syncs with your phone. The transcript has a search field.
- Volume boost now works: with it on, the volume slider, wheel and arrow keys go up to 200%. It is off by default and adds no extra sound processing until you pass 100%.
- Adaptive player size now works: a vertical or narrow video gets a taller player on the watch page, capped to fit the screen. It is on by default, as on Android.
- The Skip silence row is gone. It never worked here, and a browser player can't remove silence the way Android does.
- A video picks up where you left it from half a second in, and starts over only in the last 1.5 seconds or past 98%, as on Android.
- Moving through a queue starts each video from the top. Picking one yourself still resumes it.
- A queue moves past a video that can't play and tells you, up to 2 in a row, then stops on the error.
- Flow can offer the last unfinished video when it opens, in a small card that plays it in the mini player or opens it. Continue watching mini player in Settings turns it off.
- The mini player comes in three sizes. A thin progress bar stays at the bottom of the video when the controls hide, and clicking the time switches to what's left.
- Optional frame step buttons while paused and a paid promotion label. The Save button shows Saved, Stats for nerds has more rows and Copy, and the description lists its tags and can be copied.

SETTINGS
- Settings rows now look like Flow for Android's: each row is its own surface, with a thin gap between rows and rounded group ends. Group titles are colored and no longer in capitals.
- The FlowNeuro page is now called Your taste, the same name it has on Flow for Android. It stays in the same place and works the same way.
- Settings has a search box. It finds a setting by its name, description or a related word, the same way Flow for Android ranks them, then opens the right tab and points out the row.
- A new switch in Appearance, Highlight new settings, puts a dot on settings added in an update and on their tab, for as long as you choose. It is off by default.
- Flow can follow your computer's light or dark mode, with its own theme for each mode and a choice of dark or AMOLED, under Follow system in Appearance. It is off by default.
- Appearance has a Font setting: Inter, or your system's font.
- Pause watch history stops saving what you watch, in Settings > Content & UI under Search and history. Save search history, Search suggestions, Max history size and Auto-delete history live there too, with a button to clear your searches.

LIBRARY
- Edit a playlist's name and description, and search inside any playlist, even one too long to show at once.
- Add a whole playlist to one of your own, or merge two of yours, from the playlist's menu. Playlists also sort by Longest and Shortest, and the Playlists page has a Custom order you can drag.
- A YouTube playlist now says when unavailable videos are hidden from it.
- Select many videos in a playlist to add them to the queue, add them to another playlist, download them, or remove them with Undo.
- Save any playlist or music collection as a file from its menu, and import one with Import from file on the Playlists or Albums page. The files are the same ones Flow for Android saves and opens. A music playlist imports into your Albums.
- Likes has Play, Shuffle and a sort, and you can select many to queue, download or remove them with Undo. Download all and Save as file work on what the page shows. With songs and videos both on show, Play and Save as file take the videos.
- History has a Shorts tab, Newest and Oldest first, and a year to narrow it to. A removed video can be brought back with Undo, and Delete Shorts from history clears only the Shorts.
- Hide any Library section in Settings > Content & UI.
- A new Remove watched videos switch takes videos out of Watch Later once you finish them, and opening Watch Later clears any you watched elsewhere, with Undo.

DOWNLOADS
- Sort downloads by Newest, Oldest, Largest, Title or Channel. Each shows its size and date, and a summary shows the space used and free.
- New Download videos you open and Download liked songs switches in Settings > Downloads.
- Find earlier downloads lists files Flow saved that the page has lost, by the tags in them. Tags for older downloads adds tags to MP4 and M4A files saved before Flow wrote them.
- Deleting a download now offers Undo.
- Player buttons, in Settings under Player, shows or hides each control on the player bar. The bar looks the same with all of them on, which is the default.

SHORTS
- Shorts work from the keyboard: Up and Down move between Shorts, Space or K plays and pauses, M mutes, and the arrows, J, L and the number keys seek.
- A shelf or a channel's Shorts now carries on into more Shorts when it runs out, instead of stopping. Saved Shorts still ends where your list ends.
- Home has a Shorts shelf, which Home Shorts Shelf in Settings turns off.
- More like this, in a Short's More menu, teaches your recommendations and puts similar Shorts right after it.
- A Short that links to a full video shows a chip that opens it. Double-click a Short to like it.
- A Short with several audio tracks offers them in its settings, and the equalizer now applies to Shorts.
- Subscriptions shows when the feed last refreshed and how many videos it checked. The count has its own switch.

HOME
- Home has three new chips. Mixes builds queues from videos you liked or finished, Live shows streams on now, and Watched brings back videos worth seeing again.
- A Subscriptions on Home switch keeps channels you follow out of Home. Clicking Home again scrolls to the top, then refreshes, and you can turn the refresh off.
- Cards from a channel you often watch now say so under the title. Explore says plainly when YouTube is limiting requests.
- Card menus add Play next, Details and Mark as unwatched. Home can show a list instead of a grid, from a button beside the chips or from Settings.

SHORTS
- A single Show Shorts switch in Settings hides Shorts everywhere, with an option to keep the Shorts tab on channels. Saved Shorts and history are kept.
- Hide watched videos is now separate for Home, Subscriptions and Shorts, and you choose when a video counts as watched.

SEARCH
- Searching again from your history brings back the filters you used. Shorts is now its own chip, next to All.
- New Unwatched, Watched and Recently uploaded chips, and 360, VR180, 3D and Location filters. A creator with recent uploads gets one card with their latest videos.

LIBRARY
- Subscriptions has a Refresh button that refreshes only the group you have open. Refresh subscriptions at startup now works.
- Manage subscriptions can be sorted by name or latest upload, and shows video channels and music artists apart.
- Videos a channel you follow made with someone else now reach your subscription feed. Collaborations has its own switch in Settings.
- The Subscribed menu on a channel can add it to a subscription group and hide that channel's Shorts from your feed. Channel pages have a Share button.
- Discover channels, opened from Your taste, lists channels like the ones you follow and channels you watch without following, and searches for any channel.

NOTIFICATIONS
- Remind me on an upcoming video or premiere puts it in your notifications when it starts. A reminder missed while Flow was closed arrives at the next launch.
- New Shorts from your subscriptions can be left out of alerts, with a Shorts switch in Settings. Several uploads since the last check now each get a notification.
- In the notifications panel you can save a video to Watch later and undo a removal. New and Last 7 days sections join Today, Yesterday and Earlier.

FIXES
- Scrolling up on the music bar can no longer leave an invisible player covering the app until a reload.
- Cancel on a confirmation now really cancels. Deleting a playlist, deleting a custom theme and resetting SponsorBlock stats no longer go ahead anyway. Every confirmation now opens inside the app, with a clear title and button.
- Removing a playlist from a card now always asks first. Deleting a playlist you made no longer offers an Undo, which only brought it back empty.
- Scrolling with the wheel over the video on the Watch page now scrolls the page, so you no longer have to move the pointer off the video first.
- Restarting Flow after an update no longer opens the link Flow was first started with a second time.
- A restarted AppImage starts from your own environment instead of the old bundle's paths.
- Flow opens in your theme from the first frame, instead of showing the default colours for a moment.
- Downloading a Short now works, from the Shorts player and from a Shorts card. The download window names portrait videos by their real resolution, so a 1080x1920 Short is 1080p, and starts on it.
- Search no longer shows videos and Shorts from channels you blocked, the channels themselves, or videos you marked Not interested.
- Trending Region is now Content country, and search and recommendations follow it as well as trending. Before, only Explore, charts and Shorts did. YouTube Music's home still follows where you connect from.
- Proxy settings now work. HTTP, SOCKS5 and SOCKS4 proxies, with an optional sign-in, carry video, music, downloads, thumbnails, sync servers and update checks. Devices on your network stay direct, and changes apply to new connections.
- Retry with Proxy on a video that won't load now really does that, and only shows when you have set a proxy up.
