FLOW DESKTOP CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 0.1.0-beta5
DATE: 2026-11-18
STATUS: PRE-RELEASE

ABOUT
Flow Desktop is a privacy-first YouTube and YouTube Music client - no account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry. It is the desktop companion to Flow for Android.

BACKUPS AND IMPORT
- Fixed Flow closing while it read a Google Takeout export with a folder named in Russian, Ukrainian, Greek, Turkish, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese or Korean, such as My Activity (#661).

SYNC
- Try again on the keychain card now asks the keychain again. Before, a keychain that could not be reached once, for example KDE Wallet starting after Flow, stayed unreachable until Flow restarted (#659). Last.fm's Allow access button gets the same fix.
- The keychain card says what went wrong and what to do on your system: on Linux it names KDE Wallet and GNOME Keyring, and it mentions macOS only on a Mac.
- Flow no longer opens the keychain at launch on a device that is in no sync group.
